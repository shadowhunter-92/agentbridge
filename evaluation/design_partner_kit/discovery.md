# Discovery interview — 20-minute draft

Ask permission to take notes first. Do not record audio/video without separate
explicit consent. Avoid customer names, credentials and personal data that are
not needed. This script has not received legal review.

| Minutes | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 | What is your role? May we take private notes and follow up? |
| 2–6 | What agent/tool workflow runs today? What can it change, and who authorizes it? |
| 6–10 | Describe the last uncertain result, duplicate or approval failure. What did it cost and how did you reconstruct it? |
| 10–14 | Which IAM/gateway/approval/audit tools already solve parts of it? What remains missing? |
| 14–17 | Who owns this problem and budget? Who can approve integration/testing? What blocks adoption? |
| 17–20 | Would a bounded synthetic demonstration answer a useful question? What would success or failure mean? May we send one agreed follow-up? |

Do not claim to have heard the same pain from other organizations unless a
real consented interview supports it. Do not promise integrations or security
guarantees beyond the selected source's evidence. Treat a polite positive
answer as interest, not purchasing intent.

Optional follow-up draft (send only with applicable authorization and consent):

> Thank you for describing [actual workflow]. Our notes are [brief factual
> summary]. Please correct anything inaccurate. If useful, the next proposed
> step is [bounded synthetic evaluation] against [exact source], with [specific
> evidence] and no production access. No booking or purchase is implied.
