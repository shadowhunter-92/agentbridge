# Supervised design-partner kit — November 13, 2026

**NOT STARTED for external acceptance.** Templates below prepare a discussion;
they are not an executed pilot, contract, legal advice or compliance opinion.
Qualified legal review and explicit agreement by the parties are required
before use as binding terms.

Use the [pinned synthetic demo](../external_validator_kit/README.md) to learn
whether a real approval/uncertain-outcome/evidence problem exists. The
maintainer does not claim independent installs, buyer demand or a paid pilot
from internal test success.

1. Hold a consented 20-minute discovery conversation using
   [the script](discovery.md). Ask about the current process, not an imagined sale.
2. Agree on [scope and consent](scope_and_consent_draft.md) before any test.
   Use synthetic local targets initially; no live recipe, payment, customer
   record, credential or autonomous production mutation.
3. Record findings in the [results template](results.md). Preserve negative
   evidence and any decision not to continue.

Targets remain 3 unrelated developer evaluations, 5 qualified buyer interviews,
an independent security review, separately administered destination/readback
and one written supervised evaluation agreement. These are separate gates.
A quote, invitation, meeting, technical interest or unpaid discussion is not a
paid pilot or completed acceptance.
