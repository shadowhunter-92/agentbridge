## Invitation scope

Ask for a 15–20 minute discussion and a voluntary, synthetic/read-only
offline SDK check. Require no credentials. Share the public README,
issue #36 and draft evaluation kit. Explicitly acknowledge experimental
alpha, limited transport coverage, and no production certification.

Do not open unrelated technical issues on their repositories for marketing.
Use published community channels or an individually verified professional
contact address, and respect their community policies. Send one relevant
invitation and at most one respectful follow-up. Record response and
consent before sharing private files.

## Feedback ledger

Track candidate, contact source, date, channel, consent, actual integration
attempt, minutes-to-first-proof, blockers, benchmark against existing gateway,
pilot suitability, and evidence supporting willingness to pay. No outreach
or reply is to be marked complete until a real transmission or response
is confirmed.
