The problem

Agents talk in different dialects — and nobody's watching the conversation.

Every protocol speaks differently, so teams hand-wire each pair. And once agents start calling each other and your tools, there's no cost control and no record of who did what — right as regulators start asking.

01 — fragmentation

Six protocols, no common ground

MCP, A2A, ACP, OpenAI & Gemini function-calling, AGNTCY — each a different shape. Bridges built "from the spec" quietly break on real agents.

02 — no control

Agents spend with no leash

Once agents call tools and each other, there's no budget, no approval gate, no identity behind the call. Costs and blast radius are unbounded.

03 — no proof

No audit when it matters

When something goes wrong — or an auditor asks — there's no tamper-evident record of what each agent did. The EU AI Act now requires one.
